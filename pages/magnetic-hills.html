A stretch of road in Ladakh appears to pull vehicles uphill. The explanation is less supernatural, and much more interesting.

MAGNETIC HILL · LADAKH, INDIA
🚙
APPARENT UPHILL
01 / THE EXPERIENCE

A road that seems to climb.

From a particular viewpoint, the surrounding slopes and horizon can make a downhill stretch look like an uphill climb.

02 / THE EXPLANATION

Your eyes are being fooled.

The effect is an optical illusion, often called a gravity hill. The visual horizon and nearby slopes can distort our sense of what is level.

03 / THE SCIENCE

Reference points matter.

We judge slope using the landscape around us. When that landscape is misleading, a road that descends can appear to rise.

THE SCIENCE BEHIND THE MYSTERY

No magic required.

01

Visual context

Our brain estimates slopes relative to the horizon and nearby terrain.

02

False horizon

Surrounding hills can make the true horizon difficult to judge accurately.

03

Gravity still works

Vehicles roll according to the actual slope and physical forces, not the apparent one.
